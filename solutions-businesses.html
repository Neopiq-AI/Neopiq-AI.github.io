Solutions · SMBs and professional-service firms

Routine Microsoft administration, done by conversation and approved by you

Your company runs on Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft 365 and perhaps Microsoft Power Platform, and the person who administers it also has another job. ChatAdmin answers the questions and runs the routine changes that otherwise mean a search through four admin centres — and it will not do anything you did not approve.

See ChatAdmin in Action Talk to us

What it is for

Simple administration

Say what you need in your own words. You do not need to know which admin centre a task lives in, or which Microsoft product boundary it crosses.

Environment-aware answers and actions

ChatAdmin reads your real environment before it proposes anything: who exists, which groups, which seats are free, which team. Its answers are about your tenant, not about Microsoft documentation.

Human control

Every change stops at a card you read and approve. Five minutes of silence declines it. Nothing is approved by software.

Less dependency on specialist knowledge

The joiner, the leaver, the shared mailbox, the access question: the routine work that normally waits for the one person who knows where it is.

Executable outcomes

Not a recommendation to go and do it: the change is executed after your click and read back before it is reported.

What it does not promise

ChatAdmin does not replace IT expertise. It has ceilings in code, it asks when a decision matters, and it tells you when something needs a person in the portal.

The workflows the product runs today

Each is a request the product handles now, operated by Neopiq in the environment it is connected to. Reads need no approval; every change is a card.
