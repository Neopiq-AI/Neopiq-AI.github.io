Entry point · the stages of access

Try with Microsoft: what the stages are, and which one you can use today

Signing in to ChatAdmin and allowing ChatAdmin to administer your Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft 365 or Microsoft Power Platform environment are different trust decisions, and we keep them separate. There is no sign-in button on this page yet: self-service sign-in with your Microsoft work account is coming, and this page says so plainly rather than offering a button that leads nowhere.

The stages

  1. Demonstration on a sample environment available now

    Three scripted scenarios over the fictional tenant “Contoso Example”, in the real interaction pattern: intent, what ChatAdmin inspected, the proposed change, a card you click, simulated execution, a verified-looking result with an audit line. Not connected to your Microsoft environment; nothing can change anything.

    See ChatAdmin in Action

  2. Sign in with your Microsoft work account coming

    What will happen: you are sent to Microsoft’s sign-in and come back signed in to ChatAdmin. What ChatAdmin learns: your name, your sign-in name and your organisation’s tenant id. Is administration access requested? No. Only identity scopes are requested; no permission on your directory, your mail or your resources. What does not happen yet: nothing is read or changed in your tenant; signing in does not connect your environment and does not replace the approval key. The explanation will be shown before the redirect, in these words.

  3. Explore with your Microsoft environment planned

    When you choose to work with your real environment, ChatAdmin asks for the specific read permissions a capability needs and explains each one: what it requests, why it is required, which feature uses it, whether you or an administrator must consent, and what ChatAdmin still cannot do. If you cannot grant it yourself, you get a clean path to ask your administrator, not an error. Read permissions are not described as harmless merely because they cannot modify data.

  4. Enable controlled execution planned

    Execution permissions are requested when you are ready to use the matching capability, explained the same way, and connected to a capability you can name. The flow: you ask for a change, ChatAdmin explains what needs to change, says why extra authorisation is needed, obtains it, returns to the pending operation, presents the approval, executes, verifies, reports.

  5. Expand organisational and integration capability planned

    Background operation, broader application permissions and platform integrations are a higher trust level and stay out of first use.

What you can do today

  • See it. The demonstration needs no account.
  • Evaluate it in an operated environment. ChatAdmin runs in a Neopiq-operated environment, connected by a Neopiq operator to one Microsoft tenant; evaluators use it through a link and the approver keys. Talk to us and we will say which stage is available for your case.
  • Be told when sign-in opens. Say so at contact@neopiq.ai.

Microsoft Marketplace: planned — the Marketplace route and this page will lead to the same activation path once it exists.
