Entry point · the stages of access

Try with Microsoft: what the stages are, and which one you can use today

Signing in to ChatAdmin and allowing ChatAdmin to administer your Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft 365 or Microsoft Power Platform environment are different trust decisions, and we keep them separate. Sign-in with a Microsoft work account is live on ChatAdmin, and today it admits Neopiq’s own organisation, where evaluations run. Connecting your own organisation is in development. There is no sign-in button on this page, because a button that ends in a refusal is worse than a sentence that says so.

The stages

  1. Demonstration on a sample environment available now

    Three scripted scenarios over the fictional tenant “Contoso Example”, in the real interaction pattern: intent, what ChatAdmin inspected, the proposed change, a card you click, simulated execution, a verified-looking result with an audit line. Not connected to your Microsoft environment; nothing can change anything.

    See ChatAdmin in Action

  2. Sign in with your Microsoft work account available now

    What happens: you are sent to Microsoft’s sign-in and come back signed in to ChatAdmin; the sign-in is required. What ChatAdmin learns: your name, your sign-in name and your organisation’s tenant id, shown on every card and recorded with every decision. Is administration access requested? No. Only identity scopes are requested; no permission on your directory, your mail or your resources; the sign-in application holds no secret and no token is kept. What does not happen: nothing is read or changed in your tenant; signing in does not connect your environment. The explanation is shown before the redirect, in these words. Who can sign in today: Neopiq’s own organisation, where evaluations run; other organisations are admitted when the next stage ships.

  3. Connect your organisation in development

    A verified administrator of your Microsoft tenant connects your organisation — nothing is installed in your tenant, no Azure subscription is needed, and Microsoft 365 with Microsoft Entra is enough to start; Azure and Power Platform are added when you use them. That administrator starts as Standard + ChatAdmin administrator (or chooses Observe or Custom) and starts working; nothing has to be configured first. ChatAdmin then asks for the specific permissions a capability needs when you first use it and explains each one before Microsoft’s consent screen: what it requests, why, which feature uses it, reading or changing, whether you or an administrator must consent — with a clean path to ask your administrator, not an error. It acts with your own Microsoft rights and never more; every change is approved by your own sign-in, bound to that one transaction; no Neopiq key exists in your organisation. The model in full.

  4. Grow without migrating planned

    A second person brings Users & Access and Activity pages for your administrator (in development); several managed tenants bring a visible tenant switcher with per-tenant access, and a second Neopiq product an app switcher (planned). Background operation and platform integrations are a higher trust level and stay out of first use. The ChatAdmin workspace will live at app.neopiq.ai; there is nothing to open there today.

What you can do today

  • See it. The demonstration needs no account.
  • Evaluate it in an operated environment. ChatAdmin runs in a Neopiq-operated environment, connected by a Neopiq operator to one Microsoft tenant; an evaluation runs signed in with Microsoft and with the approver keys — today the sign-in admits Neopiq’s own organisation, so how you take part is agreed in the conversation that starts the evaluation. Talk to us and we will say which stage is available for your case.
  • Be told when your organisation can connect. Say so at contact@neopiq.ai.

Microsoft Marketplace: planned — the Marketplace route and this page will lead to the same activation path once it exists.
