2 Sign in and start

IT service providers (MSP)

Select the trial on the MSP card and sign in with your Microsoft work account. Give your company name and how many customers you manage, and your free trial starts at once. No payment method.

One organisation (Standard or Essential)

Select Get started on your plan’s card and sign in with your Microsoft work account. Until online buying opens, we set up your plan with you directly: contact@neopiq.ai.

Signing in identifies you: ChatAdmin learns your name, sign-in name and tenant. It reads and changes nothing in your tenant. ChatAdmin pauses when nobody uses it, so the first visit after a quiet spell can take up to half a minute.

3 Connect your tenant

A Global Administrator of your tenant accepts Microsoft’s consent for the permissions ChatAdmin uses to read; some areas ask for their own consent the first time you use them. Nothing is installed, and no Neopiq key or secret is kept in your tenant. ChatAdmin then reads with your own Microsoft rights, never more.

Not a Global Administrator yourself? ChatAdmin gives you an address to send to one. It holds nothing secret and grants nothing.

IT service providers connect each customer the same way: ChatAdmin makes a connection link for the customer’s administrator, and every customer stays separate.

Questions

Do I need a payment method for the trial?

No. The free trial for IT service providers needs no payment method.

What does signing in give ChatAdmin?

Your name, your sign-in name and your tenant id. Signing in asks for no administrative access and reads or changes nothing in your tenant.

Can ChatAdmin change things in my tenant?

Not yet. In your tenant ChatAdmin reads and explains, and gives you the steps for a change in the Microsoft admin center. When changes open, each one waits on an Approve / Reject card that a person decides.

Where is my data kept, and for how long?

Conversations are kept in Neopiq’s own Microsoft Azure storage, with anything that looks like a key or password removed, and deleted after 90 days. More on security.
