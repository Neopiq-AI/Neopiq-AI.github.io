Talk to us. Choosing the right address saves you a day.

Neopiq builds ChatAdmin, which administers Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Power Platform by conversation. The routes below are all of them — there is no ticket portal, no chat widget and deliberately no contact form: a form would mean collecting your details through a site that otherwise collects nothing, and an e-mail leaves you in control of what you send.

The routes, by what you need

Evaluate ChatAdmin, or a partner conversation

contact@neopiq.ai. An operated evaluation in a connected environment; an MSP or IT service provider wanting to operate it; a distributor or channel partner; an integration conversation; arguments with how the approval gate is designed. A person reads it and answers with which stage is available for your case.

Support — something is not working

support@neopiq.ai, Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00 CET, first response targeted within one business day — a target, not a contractual SLA. What to include is on the support page.

Security — you found a vulnerability

contact@neopiq.ai with a subject line starting SECURITY; call as well if it is actively dangerous. The trust page says what to include and how reports are handled.

Phone — when writing is too slow

0041 78 223 6716. A person answers when a person can. For anything involving detail worth getting right, e-mail first so the call has something in writing to work from.

About getting access to ChatAdmin

There is no self-service sign-up today. ChatAdmin runs in a Neopiq-operated environment, connected by a Neopiq operator to one Microsoft tenant; an evaluation starts with a conversation, on purpose: a tool that makes real changes in a Microsoft environment should not be handed out by a web page before anyone has discussed scope, controls and what the permissions page means for you. Sign in with Microsoft is live for Neopiq’s own organisation; connecting your own organisation is in development; a Microsoft Marketplace listing is planned. Say what you want at contact@neopiq.ai and we will tell you exactly where it stands.

The demonstration, by contrast, needs no contact at all.

You are also welcome not to contact us

The demonstration answers its own questions, the documentation answers most others, and both work without telling us anything.
