Reaching us is one e-mail. Choosing the right address saves you a day.

Neopiq builds ChatAdmin, which administers Microsoft Azure by conversation, and the Planning Data Auditor for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management. Whichever brought you here, the routes below are all of them — there is no ticket portal, no chat widget, and deliberately no contact form: a form would mean collecting your details through a site that otherwise collects nothing, and an e-mail leaves you in control of what you send.

The routes, by what you need

General — anything without a better address

contact@neopiq.ai. Questions about either product, interest in ChatAdmin, findings from the free check you want challenged or interpreted, partnerships, press, arguments with how the approval gate is designed. A person reads it.

Support — something is not working

support@neopiq.ai, Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00 CET, first response targeted within one business day — a target, not a contractual SLA. What to include, and what is and is not covered for the free tool, is on the support page.

contact@neopiq.ai with a subject line starting SECURITY; call as well if it is actively dangerous. The security page says what to include and how such reports are handled.

About getting access to ChatAdmin

There is no self-service sign-up for ChatAdmin today. No trial button, no account creation — access starts with a conversation, on purpose: a tool that makes real changes to a cloud subscription should not be handed out by a web page before anyone has discussed scope, controls, and what the permissions page means for your environment. If you want ChatAdmin pointed at your estate, or simply want to be told when that opens, say so at contact@neopiq.ai and we will tell you exactly where it stands.

The free check, by contrast, needs no contact at all: it runs in your browser, requires no account and no conversation, and we never see that you ran it.

You are also welcome not to contact us

The free check answers its own questions, the documentation answers most others, and both work without telling us anything.
