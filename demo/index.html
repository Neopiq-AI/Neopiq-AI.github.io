What the real product does differently

Real cards are approved by a signed-in person

In the product you are signed in with Microsoft, and the decision is recorded with your name. In the Neopiq-operated environment Approve also asks for that Microsoft area’s approver key, handed over on a different channel and typed by the human into the page; in your own organisation your own sign-in is the approval and no key exists (in development). Here the buttons simply work. A real card also declines itself after five minutes of silence; here it waits.

Real execution re-reads Microsoft

After every approved step the product reads the object back from Microsoft Graph, Azure Resource Manager or the Exchange Online administration endpoint and shows that state under “Verified after change”. Here the verified lines are scripted, and every result says simulated.

Real answers come from your environment

The product reads the real users, groups, seats, teams and resources of the environment it is connected to, and understands your own words. Here three conversations are scripted over a fictional tenant, and free text only picks the nearest one.
