How it stays safe

  • ChatAdmin proposes; a person approves. Every change waits on an Approve / Reject card, and nothing is approved by software.
  • An unanswered card declines itself after five minutes. Every result is read back from Microsoft before it is reported.
  • Signing in reads and changes nothing in your tenant: your identity only, no administrative access.
  • In your own organisation it will act with your own Microsoft rights, never more, with no Neopiq key or secret kept there. in development
