Support: one inbox, a person behind it, and no pretend service level

Neopiq is a small company, and the honest version of support at a small company is a named channel, stated hours, and a response target we actually intend to hit — not a promise dressed up as a contract. This page is that version, for ChatAdmin, which administers Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Power Platform by conversation.

The commitment, stated exactly

Where and when

Write to support@neopiq.ai. The inbox is read Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET. We aim to send a first substantive response — not an auto-acknowledgement — within one business day.

What that target is, and is not

One business day is a target, not a contractual SLA. We publish it because it is what we hold ourselves to; we do not attach penalties, uptime percentages or response statistics to it, because we have none to show you yet and will not invent any. If your procurement process requires a contractual service level, say so in your first e-mail and we will tell you plainly whether we can offer one.

If it is urgent

Call 0041 78 223 6716. A person answers when a person can; outside the hours above, e-mail first so the details are in writing and the call has something to work from.

What to put in a report, so the first reply is useful

For any report

  • What you did — the request you typed, in order
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • The exact text of the answer or error, copied rather than paraphrased
  • When it happened, with your timezone

ChatAdmin-specific details that help

  • The Microsoft area and the action name as they appear on the card or in the tool chip
  • Whether the card was approved, rejected or timed out
  • What the header said: the environment, live or simulated, the model status
  • Never an approver key, a token or a password — we will never ask for one, and anyone who does is not us

Security problems take a different door

If you have found a vulnerability — in this website or in ChatAdmin — do not put it in the general support queue. The trust page says exactly how to report it and what happens next.
