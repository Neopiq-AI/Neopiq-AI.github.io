Starting ChatAdmin…

ChatAdmin pauses when nobody is using it, so the first visit after a quiet spell can take up to half a minute while it starts. This page moves on by itself as soon as ChatAdmin answers; there is nothing to click.

There you sign in with your Microsoft work account. Signing in identifies you and reads or changes nothing in your tenant; you land on the plan choice and the activation checklist.
