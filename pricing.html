Pricing

Two things to know. The free check is free — Planning Data Auditor, for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, runs in your browser, needs no account, and that does not change. Everything else — ChatAdmin and the deeper Dynamics 365 work — is commercial, and pricing is on request. The commercial side starts with a conversation, not a checkout.

Available today · no account

Planning Data Auditor — the free check

Free

For anyone who wants to find out whether the findings are worth acting on, before talking to a vendor. Dynamics 365 only — administering a cloud has no local, read-only form, so there is no free-check equivalent for Azure.

  • The full local check, unlimited: every one of the 26 Dynamics 365 planning and master data checks, on as many extracts as you like
  • Nothing transferred: it runs in your browser, so there is no account, no upload and nothing for IT to approve
  • Findings you can act on: named items, the numbers behind each claim, impact, recommended change and the owner

Run the free check

No sign-up step, because there is nothing to sign up to. Where your data is while it runs.

Commercial · starts with a conversation

Everything beyond the check

Pricing on request

For organisations that want more than a list of findings: work explained, prioritised and — on the Azure side — executed inside their own subscription.

  • ChatAdmin: Azure administered by conversation, with an Approve / Reject gate before every change, designed to run inside your subscription. There is no self-service sign-up today — access starts with a conversation
  • Findings written up, not just listed: the commercial side of the Planning Data Auditor pillar covers explanation, prioritisation and reporting on top of the free check, through to supervised repair inside your own environment
  • Compute stays on your Microsoft bill, not ours — which is also why your data does not have to move to us

Start the conversation

A person reads contact@neopiq.ai and answers 0041 78 223 6716. You will get a straight answer about what ChatAdmin can and cannot do — its limits are published so you can judge that before writing.

Why the local check is free, and stays free

It runs on your hardware, so it costs us nothing to serve. Metering it would only slow down the one thing that makes this business work: you finding out that the findings are real before anybody has to ask permission for anything. It stays free whatever happens on the commercial side, and it stays unlimited.

Why the expensive part runs on your Azure bill

The heavy work is designed to run inside your own subscription. That means the compute is billed to you by Microsoft on the agreement you already have, at the price you already negotiated — and it means your data never has to move to us for the heavy work to happen. We orchestrate; you own the environment.

That is the model on both pillars, and for ChatAdmin it is the whole point: it is designed to execute inside your subscription, on your identity and your Microsoft bill, rather than reaching into your estate from a service of ours.

VAT

Neopiq GmbH is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged. There is no VAT number to quote. Any price we quote is the whole amount — nothing is added to it and nothing is included in it.

The useful part costs nothing today

That is the order we chose: be useful first, and ask for money only when there is something worth paying for.
