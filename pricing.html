Pricing

One tier for each step of the product. Free is the Level 0 tier, Trial is the Level 1 tier, Enterprise is the Level 2 tier. Each tier fully unlocks the one step it is named for and includes everything in the tier below it. Only Level 0 exists today, so only the Free tier is open — and the free part is deliberately the part that has to earn everything after it.

Why the local check is free, and stays free

It runs on your hardware, so it costs us nothing to serve. Metering it would only slow down the one thing that makes this business work: you finding out that the findings are real before anybody has to ask permission for anything. Every paid tier includes it unlimited, because every tier includes everything in the tier below.

Why the expensive part runs on your Azure bill

The heavy work at Level 2 is planned to run inside your own subscription. That means the compute is billed to you by Microsoft on the agreement you already have, at the price you already negotiated — and it means your data never has to move to us for the heavy work to happen. We orchestrate; you own the environment. It is not built yet.

What you are not paying for

No implementation project, no per-report development cycle, and no seat licences for people who only want to read a finding. If any of that changes as the commercial model is decided, this page changes with it.
